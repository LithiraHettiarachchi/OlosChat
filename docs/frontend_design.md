# OlosChat – Design System & Visual Guidelines

## Product Identity

**Product:** OlosChat  
**Category:** AI-powered chat & productivity application  
**Tone:** Calm, intelligent, trustworthy, premium  
**Core Feeling:**  
> Clear like the ocean, focused like a conversation, premium without being loud.

OlosChat prioritizes readability, low cognitive load, and subtle elegance over visual noise.

---

## Color Palette

### Brand Colors

**Primary Ocean Blue**  
`#0B5ED7`  
- Primary actions (Send, Continue, Confirm)
- Active states
- Links & focus indicators
- Chat highlights

**Ocean Blue – Hover**  
`#0A53BE`

**Ocean Blue – Soft**
- `#0B5ED7/15`
- `#0B5ED7/10`
- `#0B5ED7/5`  
Used for message bubbles, active backgrounds, and subtle emphasis.

---

### Accent Color

**Gold Accent**  
`#D4AF37`  
- Premium indicators
- Badges (Pro / AI / Featured)
- Important highlights (used sparingly)

**Gold Soft**
- `#D4AF37/20`
- `#D4AF37/10`  
Used for subtle borders, glow effects, and emphasis.
